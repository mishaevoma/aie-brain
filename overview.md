# Overview

The mental model this brain works from, as of 22 November 2026. It is a synthesis of the pages it links to. When a concept page changes the picture, update this page too.

## The stack

Six layers, and three things that cut across them. Use these names when deciding where a problem or a change belongs.

| Layer | What it is | Who changes it | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product | What the user is actually getting done | You | [Building an agent product](playbooks/building-an-agent-product.md) |
| Interface | Where a person meets the agent: CLI, app, chat | You or a vendor | [Harness](concepts/harness.md) |
| Context layer | Instruction files, skills, MCP servers, memory | You | [Context layer](concepts/context-layer.md) |
| Harness | Loop, tools, context management, gate, sandbox | A vendor, or you | [Harness](concepts/harness.md) |
| Agent platform | What keeps deployed agents running: session record, loop host, sandboxes, tool gateway, triggers, queue | You, or a vendor | [Agent platforms](concepts/agent-platforms.md) |
| Model | The weights | A lab | |

Across all of them:

- **Checking**: [verification](concepts/verification.md) while the work happens, [evals](concepts/evals.md) across many runs.
- **Containment**: [permissions and sandboxing](concepts/sandboxing-and-permissions.md), [agent identity and tenancy](concepts/agent-identity.md), and [side effects and approvals](concepts/side-effects.md) for the actions that get through.
- **People**: [human judgment](concepts/human-judgment.md).

## What the sources agree on

1. **Start simple.** One good model call before a workflow, and a workflow before an agent. [Agents and workflows](concepts/agents-and-workflows.md)
2. **Context is a budget.** Load little, load on demand, and keep what is always loaded short. [Context engineering](concepts/context-engineering.md)
3. **Tools are written for a model.** Few, task-shaped, described like a briefing, and tested with evals. [Tool design](concepts/tool-design.md)
4. **Short instruction file, procedures in skills.** [Instruction files](concepts/instruction-files.md), [Skills](concepts/skills.md)
5. **Files first for knowledge and memory.** Add search or a database when the files stop being enough. [LLM wiki](concepts/llm-wiki.md), [Memory](concepts/memory.md)
6. **Verification is the bottleneck.** Give the agent a way to run the real thing, and accept evidence, not summaries. [Verification](concepts/verification.md)
7. **A deterministic boundary under any model-based gate.** [Permissions and sandboxing](concepts/sandboxing-and-permissions.md)
8. **Scaffolding expires.** Each piece is a bet on what the model cannot do yet. Re-test at every model release. [Harness](concepts/harness.md), [Long-running agents](concepts/long-running-agents.md)
9. **Evals start small and from real failures, and small gaps between scores mean little.** [Evals](concepts/evals.md), [Trusting eval numbers](concepts/eval-validity.md)
10. **Encode corrections, then prune.** [Compounding](concepts/compounding.md)
11. **Intent and accountability stay with a person.** [Human judgment](concepts/human-judgment.md)
12. **A long run must survive a crash or a deploy without starting over, and an action that changes the world needs a plan for running twice.** How to record the run is contested. [Durable execution](concepts/durable-execution.md), [Side effects and approvals](concepts/side-effects.md)
13. **Credentials are scoped to one run and short-lived.** Whether they stay outside the sandbox, attached at a proxy, or are injected into it is still argued. [Agent identity and tenancy](concepts/agent-identity.md), [Permissions and sandboxing](concepts/sandboxing-and-permissions.md)

## Live debates

- **Does the harness matter?** Barely, for choosing between mature coding harnesses [Theo video, 30 Oct 2026](sources/2026-10-30-theo-my-last-video-about-ai-harnesses.md). Decisively, for whether a run is useful and safe [Loams, Oct 2026](sources/2026-10-18-loams-coding-agent-harnesses.md). A lot, but temporarily, for long tasks [Harness design, Mar 2026](sources/2026-03-24-anthropic-harness-design-long-running-apps.md). See [Harness](concepts/harness.md).
- **Adopt a harness or own the loop?** Adopt one and configure it [Theo, 22 Oct 2026](sources/2026-10-22-theo-what-is-a-harness.md). Own the loop, for a customer-facing feature where you need the exact prompt and a pause before each action [12-Factor Agents, Mar 2025](sources/2025-03-30-humanlayer-12-factor-agents.md). See [Harness](concepts/harness.md).
- **Compaction or a full log?** Summarise history inside the window [Context engineering, Sep 2025](sources/2025-09-29-anthropic-context-engineering.md), or keep everything outside and choose slices at read time [Managed Agents, Apr 2026](sources/2026-04-08-anthropic-managed-agents.md). See [Context engineering](concepts/context-engineering.md).
- **One agent or several?** One thread, for work whose parts must agree [Cognition, Jun 2025](sources/2025-06-12-cognition-dont-build-multi-agents.md). A lead with parallel subagents, for wide research [Multi-agent research system, Jun 2025](sources/2025-06-13-anthropic-multi-agent-research-system.md). Small agents of a few steps each, run in sequence by ordinary code [12-Factor Agents, Mar 2025](sources/2025-03-30-humanlayer-12-factor-agents.md). For code, a poor fit in 2025 and working at scale in 2026 where the checks are strong and the work separates [C compiler, Feb 2026](sources/2026-02-05-anthropic-building-c-compiler.md) · [Osmani, 17 Nov 2026](sources/2026-11-17-osmani-claude-code-team-verification.md). See [Multi-agent systems](concepts/multi-agent-systems.md).
- **Write more down or less?** Encode nearly all domain knowledge [Cherny, 15 Jul 2026](sources/2026-07-15-cherny-automation-is-the-highest-leverage.md), and keep the always-loaded file small [Red Hat, Jul 2026](sources/2026-07-27-redhat-agents-md-and-agent-skills.md). See [Instruction files](concepts/instruction-files.md).
- **When should memory be written?** During the task or in an offline pass, and how often to re-verify it [Agent memory, Nov 2026](sources/2026-11-06-anthropic-components-of-agent-memory.md) · [Chase, Apr 2026](sources/2026-04-04-chase-continual-learning-for-ai-agents.md). See [Memory](concepts/memory.md).
- **Who keeps shared knowledge current?** A separate curator agent on a schedule, with working agents only flagging gaps [Knowledge factory, Nov 2026](sources/2026-11-21-chase-how-to-build-a-knowledge-factory.md). Or the working agent itself, which in Anthropic's tests of its newest models kept useful notes unaided [Agent memory, Nov 2026](sources/2026-11-06-anthropic-components-of-agent-memory.md). Whether a person approves what the curator writes is not settled either. See [Knowledge factory](concepts/knowledge-factory.md).
- **Evals from day one?** Yes, a few dozen tasks [Demystifying evals, Jan 2026](sources/2026-01-09-anthropic-demystifying-evals.md). Or dogfood first [Google, Sep 2026](sources/2026-09-09-google-anatomy-of-harness-engineering.md). Or read and annotate real interactions first, and let the evals come out of the failure counts [Husain field guide, Mar 2025](sources/2025-03-24-husain-field-guide-improving-ai-products.md). See [Evals](concepts/evals.md).
- **Fix eval criteria first, or let them emerge?** Fixed before any data is collected [Safety evaluations, Nov 2026](sources/2026-11-13-anthropic-building-strong-safety-evaluations.md). Found by grading real outputs, and revised [Husain field guide, Mar 2025](sources/2025-03-24-husain-field-guide-improving-ai-products.md). See [Trusting eval numbers](concepts/eval-validity.md).
- **Evals from day one?** Yes, a few dozen tasks [Demystifying evals, Jan 2026](sources/2026-01-09-anthropic-demystifying-evals.md). Or dogfood first [Google, Sep 2026](sources/2026-09-09-google-anatomy-of-harness-engineering.md). See [Evals](concepts/evals.md).
- **Checkpoints or a workflow engine?** Checkpoints at step boundaries fit agents better than a replay engine, with a task queue in front and leases and watchdogs around them [Building LangGraph, Sep 2025](sources/2025-09-04-langchain-building-langgraph.md) · [Absurd in production, Apr 2026](sources/2026-04-04-ronacher-absurd-in-production.md). They leave the supervisor, the durable retries and the single writer to you [Restate, Jun 2026](sources/2026-06-15-restate-checkpointing-is-not-resiliency.md). Durability can also sit inside the harness [Pi Durable, Oct 2026](sources/2026-10-01-earendil-pi-durable.md), whose model a workflow vendor accepts while answering that an engine adds automatic takeover by another machine [Pi on Temporal, Oct 2026](sources/2026-10-08-temporal-immortal-life-of-pi.md). See [Durable execution](concepts/durable-execution.md).
- **Build the agent platform or rent it?** Rent if one provider's models are enough, because the plumbing does not set a product apart [Plumbing wars, Apr 2026](sources/2026-04-28-proctor-plumbing-wars.md). Build, to keep the choice of model, the pace of change and the traces [Laminar, Oct 2026](sources/2026-10-20-ramp-building-laminar.md). Either way, the session a provider stores is what locks you in [Session portability, Jul 2026](sources/2026-07-30-earendil-session-you-cannot-take-with-you.md). See [Managed agent runtimes](concepts/managed-agent-runtimes.md).
- **Is the sandbox disposable?** Yes, with the loop outside it [Shopify, May 2026](sources/2026-05-28-shopify-under-the-river.md) · [Harvey, Apr 2026](sources/2026-04-07-harvey-building-spectre.md). No, it should be a durable machine that is snapshotted and woken [Code And Let Live, Jan 2026](sources/2026-01-09-fly-code-and-let-live.md) · [Cognition, Apr 2026](sources/2026-04-23-cognition-what-we-learned-building-cloud-agents.md). See [Agent platforms](concepts/agent-platforms.md).

## What this brain does not cover yet

- Anthropic published 32 of the 139 sources. The nine added after the seed are from independent practitioners, and the 79 on agent runtimes come from more than forty other publishers, many of which sell what they describe. The pages on models, context and evals still rest mostly on Anthropic and on people who build on Claude, and guidance from other labs is still nearly absent.
- Most figures were measured on Anthropic's models and products: memory gains, approval rates, classifier miss rates, retrieval failure rates. Read them as a direction, and measure again on the model you run. The exceptions so far are context rot, whose direction was measured on 18 models from four vendors ([context engineering](concepts/context-engineering.md)), and the tool counts at which selection breaks down, measured on DeepSeek and Llama models ([tool design](concepts/tool-design.md)).
- Nothing on fine-tuning, voice or multimodal products, cost and pricing, or product and interface design for AI features.
- Coding agents dominate. Other verticals appear only as examples.

A review of a product design against this brain on 17 November 2026 found four more gaps. The research of 18 November filled one and part of another:

- Filled: durable execution for tasks that wait on a person or must survive a restart. See [Durable execution](concepts/durable-execution.md) and [Side effects and approvals](concepts/side-effects.md).
- Partly filled: serving many customers from one product. [Agent identity and tenancy](concepts/agent-identity.md) rests on one production case of keeping tenants apart. Data residency, noisy neighbours and limits per customer beyond a spend cap are still missing.
- Open: model gateways: routing, falling back to another model, and what a silent fallback does to eval results.
- Open: agents that operate an existing application through its interface: browser and computer use, and tools a page declares for the agent.

The runtime pages have gaps of their own:

- Companies publish how much their background agents are used, and rarely how often a run fails or what it costs.
- Nothing measured on how well a model resolves a tool call whose outcome is unknown, and nothing on approvals that go stale while they wait.
- Calls between agents (A2A) appear mostly as documentation. One company uses an A2A client in production, but only to pass identity, with nothing on tasks or their delivery.
- Most sources on durable execution are written by vendors of an engine or a runtime.

Leads for filling these gaps are in the [watchlist](watchlist.md).
