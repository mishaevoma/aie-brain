# Context engineering

**In one line:** "Context engineering" is a better name than "prompt engineering", because what people assume it means is close to what it does mean.

## What it says

- In June 2025 the term **context engineering** was gaining ground as a replacement for "prompt engineering". The author likes it and expects it to last.
- He quotes Tobi Lütke, for whom it is the art of giving the model all the context that makes a task plausibly solvable, and Andrej Karpathy, for whom it is filling the context window with just the right information for the next step.
- "Prompt engineering" was meant to name the real difficulty of building reliable prompts. Most people took it to mean typing into a chatbot. The meaning people infer from a term is the one that sticks.

## What to do with it

- Use "context engineering" for deciding what goes into the window at each step, and "prompt engineering" for the wording of one prompt.

## Caveats

- A note of a few paragraphs, most of it quotation. It records when and why the term spread. For the practice see [Effective context engineering](2025-09-29-anthropic-context-engineering.md).
