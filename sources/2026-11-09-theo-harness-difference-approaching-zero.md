# Real world diff between harnesses is approaching 0 fast

**In one line:** Theo's shortest statement of his current view: a harness is unremarkable plumbing, and the practical difference between harnesses is close to nothing.

## What it says

- Asked whether T3 Code will ever build its own harness, he points at a new T3 Code feature (small text-generation jobs such as thread titles and commit messages sent straight to a model API instead of a coding agent) and says that technically counts as one.
- His point: harnesses are meaningless as a differentiator. If his team wrote one it would be too boring to care about. The real-world difference between harnesses is "approaching 0 fast".
- The person he replied to disagreed in the thread: a layer that talks to other harnesses will not beat one that controls the whole pipeline, because you do not control the other side.
- Another reply took it further: harness features beyond giving a capable model the right tools are debt.

## What to do with it

- Hold the claim as contested. It is one builder's judgement about coding harnesses in late 2026, not a measurement.
- Notice the small practical idea underneath: not every model call needs an agent. Simple generation jobs can go directly to a model API.

## Caveats

- A reply in a thread, with no evidence attached. Longer argument in his 30 Oct 2026 video.
- He builds a product that sits on top of other people's harnesses.
