# Obsidian as a local wiki for what agents write

**In one line:** Obsidian's CEO confirms the pattern from the reader's side: people use Obsidian to read what their agents research and write, and it works best in a vault kept separate from personal notes.

## What it says

- A growing number of people use Obsidian as a local wiki for reading material their agents produce.
- He recommends a dedicated vault for this, filled with content through tools such as the Obsidian Web Clipper.
- Posted as a quote of Karpathy's "LLM Knowledge Bases" the same day.

## What to do with it

- Keep an agent-maintained wiki in its own folder or vault, apart from notes you write yourself.
- Any markdown folder works as the store; Obsidian is only the viewer.

## Caveats

- A two-sentence post from the maker of the tool it recommends.
