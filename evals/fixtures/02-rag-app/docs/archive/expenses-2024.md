# Expenses (2024 policy)

## Filing a claim

File claims within 30 days of the purchase, using form EXP-101. Attach a receipt for anything above 10 EUR.

## Limits

Meals while travelling: up to 40 EUR per day. Hotels: up to 150 EUR per night. Economy class for all flights.

## What is not covered

Fines, minibar charges, and personal phone contracts.
