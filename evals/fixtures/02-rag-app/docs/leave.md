# Leave

## Annual leave

Full-time staff get 25 days of annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Leave is requested in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for absences longer than three days. Up to five unused days carry over to the next year and must be taken by 31 March.

## Sick leave

Tell your manager before your first meeting of the day. A doctor's note is needed from the fourth consecutive day. Sick days are not deducted from annual leave.

## Parental leave

Either parent may take up to 16 weeks at full pay within the first year. Use form LV-110 and give eight weeks' notice where possible.
