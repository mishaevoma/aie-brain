# Expenses (2026 policy)

This policy replaces the 2024 policy from 1 January 2026.

## Filing a claim

File claims in the finance portal within 60 days of the purchase, using form EXP-204. Attach an itemised receipt for anything above 20 EUR. Claims filed late need approval from your department head.

## Limits

Meals while travelling: up to 55 EUR per day. Hotels: up to 180 EUR per night, 240 EUR in London, Zurich and New York. Economy class for flights under six hours.

## What is not covered

Fines, minibar charges, and personal phone contracts. Home-office equipment has its own budget; see form EXP-310.
